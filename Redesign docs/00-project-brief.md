### Conversion psychology
- **Specificity > generality:** "11 engagements" not "many clients"

- **Process transparency:** Show the 4-layer architecture so buyer knows what they're getting

- **Anchoring:** Show the engagement model before pricing discussion
- **Loss aversion:** Frame around what they lose by NOT having production-grade AI (audit failures, demo-to-prod gap)
