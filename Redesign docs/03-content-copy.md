## Copy rules
- Same positioning language everywhere (see 07-seo-aeo.md for exact wording)
- No exclamation marks. No "revolutionize." No "cutting-edge." No "leverage."
- State what you do. Show proof. Move on.
- Every page's first paragraph must be understandable by an LLM in isolation.

### Architecture
```
Heading: Inside the engine.

Intro: Every Stallwart build moves through the same four layers. A governance guarantee in one system is a governance guarantee in all of them.

Layer 1 — Intelligence
Where a judgment is made. Models selected and constrained for the decision at hand, calibrated to report their own uncertainty rather than assert through it.

Layer 2 — Orchestration
Where work is routed. State, retries, and sequencing. The layer that makes a system run unattended instead of needing a person to advance it.

Layer 3 — Governance
Where limits are enforced. Policy expressed as runtime controls, not documentation. Approval gates, escalation paths, and an audit trail written as work happens.

Layer 4 — Production
Where it meets reality. Observability, rollback, and load behavior. The difference between a system that demonstrated well and a system that is still correct on a Tuesday.
```

Q: How long does a typical engagement take?
A: Weeks, not months. Every engagement has a committed build date agreed before work begins.

Q: Who owns the work product?
A: You do. Source code, infrastructure as code, runbooks, and documentation — all yours. No lock-in, no dependency on Stallwart to keep it running.

Q: What industries does Stallwart serve?
A: SaaS, Fintech, Healthcare, Logistics, Marketplaces, Operations, Agencies, and B2B companies. Any team that needs production-grade AI or custom software.

Q: Where is Stallwart based?
A: India. We work with clients globally.

Q: How is Stallwart different from other AI development companies?
A: Three things. First, every build runs through the same 4-layer architecture — governance and auditability are engineered in, not bolted on. Second, fixed-price engagements with full ownership — no hourly billing, no lock-in. Third, we say no when off-the-shelf already solves the problem.

Q: What is the Stallwart 4-layer architecture?
A: Every Stallwart build passes through four layers: Intelligence (where AI makes a judgment), Orchestration (where work is routed without human intervention), Governance (where policies are enforced at runtime), and Production (where reliability is maintained under real conditions).
